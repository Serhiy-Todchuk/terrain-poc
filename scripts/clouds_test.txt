# middle / high layers at their own altitudes, drifting with the HRRR winds,
# shading the scene; the time blend between hours; fog off; the sun at the
# weather time (sunrise, sunset, night); live weather at the end.
# Replays fixed hours of 2026-10-10/11 (disk cache once seen; NOMADS keeps ~2 days).
